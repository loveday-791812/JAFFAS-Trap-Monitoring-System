Dashboard Help

This page shows catch activity across all traps

  • Date Range — switch between last 7 days, Last 30 days, or All time.
  • KPI cards — quick totals for the selected range.
  • Table headers — click any column (Trap no, catch date, etc.) to sort by it. Click again to reverse the order.
  • Download JSON — saves the currently shown data as a file.
  • Groups — use + New group to put traps you look after together, then pick the group to see only those traps. Your groups are saved to your account and only you can see them.
