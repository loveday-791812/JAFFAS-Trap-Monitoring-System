Terms & Conditions

TrapWatch is solely for use with wireless systems supplied by Farm & Forest Telemetry. As physical and atmospheric conditions may interfere with transmission, there is no guarantee that all signals will be recorded on the system. Contact fftelemetry1@gmail.com if you have any questions. TrapWatch is run by Farm & Forest Telemetry, to monitor pest traps. When you sign up we collect your email address and the role you ask for, and we record your sign-ins and account changes. We use them to give you access, send the reports you choose and keep the system secure. If you save trap groups on the Dashboard, only you can see them in TrapWatch. Farm & Forest Telemetry's TrapWatch admins can see your details. Our service providers, Google Firebase and Resend, store them for us in Singapore and the United States. You need to give an email address to have an account. You can change your reports or delete your account in Settings. To see everything we hold about you, or to ask for a correction, email fftelemetry1@gmail.com.
